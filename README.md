`main.ts` writes a single 118-character sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, explaining the modern internet to someone who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — using only words coined before their time. A library stands for all the world's knowledge; a letter stands for instant messages. No list of features, just one picture a person from 1800 could hold in their head:

> Picture a library holding every book in the world, where a letter you write reaches its reader in the blink of an eye.
